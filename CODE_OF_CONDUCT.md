# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation. Apply these standards consistently and explain moderation decisions without revealing private information. For a confidential safety or privacy concern, use the repository's private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md. Do not include personal information in public issues.
